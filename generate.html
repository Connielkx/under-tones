GENERATE YOUR OWN SOUNDSCAPE

From street image to synthetic audio.

This is not the full-scale predictive model used in the UNDER-tones project. It is a snapshot of the process behind how it works: visual evidence, inferred sound-source categories, then a synthetic listening scenario. See the full method.

Upload a photograph of an urban street. UNDER-tones identifies visible environmental features and uses them to assemble an illustrative 30-second soundscape. The result is an interpretation of potential sound sources, not a recording or acoustic prediction of the location.
